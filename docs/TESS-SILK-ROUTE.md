# Tess’s connected silk route

October 14, 2026. [Issue #53](https://github.com/haydenwatkins/Nobodys-Quest/issues/53). Design and isolated implementation after Pillar’s source freeze; no release claim.

Follow the traveling bead toward a silk junction. A real free attack unhooks one outgoing strand before the bead commits there; its sister strand carries the next segment. Walk the slack route toward Tess. The current segment finishes, and the bead remains active on the selected connection. Choices relocate pressure rather than deleting a whole act. Ordinary walking has no global slowdown or pull; it remains a full alternative.

One fork introduces the connection and its decision window. A crossing fork changes the approach in phase two. The last phase connects two junction decisions through one braid. Terrain-clear, reachable paths carry the real swept bead; warnings match its eight-pixel foot-contact radius. A committed junction retires its choice props. Expiry, ward interrupt, phase, defeat, travel or knockout retires transient silk. No lasting map mutation, new control, currency or compulsory task.

The native 90 health/eight-point Dark ward and body speed remain. Both help choices remain independent and gifts equal. Thread Mark/star ground collection, restored silver roads, Silkstep’s discovery/outing/chosen return, owned-region quiet and earned Manyfold revisits retain existing consumers. All verification gates remain pending; supplied checkpoints and physical-device limits will be recorded separately.
