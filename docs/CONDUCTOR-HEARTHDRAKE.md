## Action and presentation rules

Each repair checks the actual contacts of **one** native chain or melee swing. A distant cast, single Storm Spark or separate partial casts cannot stand in for the intended action. Mechanisms are inert, reusable until repaired, and excluded from mastery, kill, multi-hit and mana rewards. There are no quotas or respawns for these repairs. Borrowed earned arts retain their normal use; no compulsory body gate was added.

Lightning and ricochet visibility use projectile collision: water blocks feet without blocking the arc; walls and trees still block it. Existing wall tests now stub that collision API instead of feet collision. Chain Lightning's description explains the same rule. Successful normal creatures retain their original hit, ward, refund and passive rules.

The shared `roadworks.opened` record adds four recognized IDs without replacing the old six. Grid cells are restored per adventure; another save cannot inherit a changed legend. Already owned forms do not gain a retroactive outing obligation. The nineteen-body discovery cooldown still asks for two native arts, two worn-body lessons and encounters in two separate clearings; world repairs add no new campaign gate.
