# Continuing upgrade plan

Updated September 18, 2026. This tracks the classic Nobody's Quest transformation; the separate Last Light brief remains its own completed assignment and review record.

## Direction
Keep the game's pixel-art personality, form mixing, exploration, and depth. Make each session rewarding through clear goals, discoveries, mastery, and build choices. Prefer meaningful improvements over adding more systems. Preserve existing saves and earned progress.

## Workstreams
1. Forms and combat: distinct handling, useful borrowed arts, visible rhythms, achievable mastery, stronger art. The recent pass covers Rat/Wizard, Ranger/Knight, Frog/Alchemist, Stormcaller/Turtle, Dragon/Mole, Vampire/Jester, Samurai/Astronomer, Druid/Weaver, Griffin/Golem, Bellkeeper/Lantern Wisp. Colossus/Riftblade are also covered, including dash mastery credit and swept hit detection. God is now covered too: area-ability behavior, Providence form-switch exploit, and art. A first description audit filled the remaining ordinary ability gaps. All 24 legendary techniques now have descriptions; all 72 trial placements were checked, with unsafe rune/opponent positions relocated using safe ground. Frog's marsh rune sequence now completes in simulation. A deeper roster-wide mastery/balance review and real playthroughs remain. These passes are improvements, not declarations that every form is finished.
2. Regions and travel: upgrade every region's routes, encounters, discoveries, rewards, landmarks, and visual identity. Recent regional work includes Sunken Marsh, Ember Ridge, Starfall, Whispering Grove, and Mistwood. Mistwood now has three persistent trail bells, a central shortcut, a one-time recovery/spirit reward, and journal guidance; routes and existing legendary sites were checked. Sunstep Prairie now offers an optional four-checkpoint courier circuit with a 45-second limit, saved personal best, and a one-time 6-spirit reward. A no-enemy Colossus movement simulation completed it without dashes in 20.36 seconds; normal combat playtesting remains. The courier circuit now has a road-verge sign and a saved, safety-gated first invitation. Windscar Canyon has distinct cliff shelves, northern guardian and southern cache approaches, with camp/exit/legend connectivity checked. Hanging Gardens now has terraced water channels, wide bridge crossings, planted borders, a southern keystone route, and a committed Old Mason recovery window. Rootdeep now has connected root chambers, silk passage markers, cocoon landmarks, and a southern silk-cache route. Tess has a visible tether boundary and committed recovery after tether/web-grid attacks. Audit remaining regions and verify entrances, return trips, checkpoints, and persistent changes.
3. Enemies and bosses: improve readable warnings, fair recovery windows, movement variety, encounter composition, and counters. Upgrade every boss in region order, pairing each region pass with its boss fight. Windscar / Sky Sovereign and Hanging Gardens / Old Mason have received paired passes. Rootdeep Hollow / Silk Matriarch now has a paired pass too. Next: Glasswater Desert exploration, then Frostbell Tundra / Bell Titan. Review the bosses in previously upgraded regions too, including Mistwood, Sunken Marsh, and Ember Ridge, before considering those regions complete. Aurelia now commits through the gust and leaves a counterattack window, with an explicit safe-lane marker.
4. Progression and replayability: connect exploration, mastery, equipment, and later-game challenges; balance rewards and costs; improve guidance without removing discovery. Check complete progression paths and cross-form builds.
5. Presentation and usability: continue authored character/environment art, effects, and game-specific menus. Prioritize phone landscape and touch, plus keyboard/controller navigation and TV legibility.
6. Release validation: test complete journeys, old saves, reloads, zone transitions, performance, and responsive layouts. Physical iPhone, iPad, Android phone, and Android TV validation remains outstanding; desktop rendering and emulation are not physical-device evidence.

## Shipping approach
Ship bounded, tested batches to main under the user's standing authorization. Include behavior tests for substantive changes and visual checks for art/layout. Follow failures and concrete playtest findings before expanding scope. Keep this plan current as priorities change.
