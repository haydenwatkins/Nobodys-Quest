# Continuing upgrade plan

Updated September 17, 2026. This tracks the classic Nobody's Quest transformation; the separate Last Light brief remains its own completed assignment and review record.

## Direction
Keep the game's pixel-art personality, form mixing, exploration, and depth. Make each session rewarding through clear goals, discoveries, mastery, and build choices. Prefer meaningful improvements over adding more systems. Preserve existing saves and earned progress.

## Workstreams
1. Forms and combat: distinct handling, useful borrowed arts, visible rhythms, achievable mastery, stronger art. The recent pass covers Rat/Wizard, Ranger/Knight, Frog/Alchemist, Stormcaller/Turtle, Dragon/Mole, Vampire/Jester, Samurai/Astronomer, Druid/Weaver, Griffin/Golem, Bellkeeper/Lantern Wisp. Colossus/Riftblade are also covered, including dash mastery credit and swept hit detection. God is now covered too: area-ability behavior, Providence form-switch exploit, and art. A first description audit filled the remaining ordinary ability gaps. All 24 legendary techniques now have descriptions; all 72 trial placements were checked, with unsafe rune/opponent positions relocated using safe ground. Frog's marsh rune sequence now completes in simulation. A deeper roster-wide mastery/balance review and real playthroughs remain. These passes are improvements, not declarations that every form is finished.
2. Regions and travel: upgrade every region's routes, encounters, discoveries, rewards, landmarks, and visual identity. Recent regional work includes Sunken Marsh, Ember Ridge, Starfall, and Whispering Grove. Audit remaining regions and verify entrances, return trips, checkpoints, and persistent changes.
3. Enemies and bosses: improve readable warnings, fair recovery windows, movement variety, encounter composition, and counters. Revisit old encounters alongside their regions.
4. Progression and replayability: connect exploration, mastery, equipment, and later-game challenges; balance rewards and costs; improve guidance without removing discovery. Check complete progression paths and cross-form builds.
5. Presentation and usability: continue authored character/environment art, effects, and game-specific menus. Prioritize phone landscape and touch, plus keyboard/controller navigation and TV legibility.
6. Release validation: test complete journeys, old saves, reloads, zone transitions, performance, and responsive layouts. Physical iPhone, iPad, Android phone, and Android TV validation remains outstanding; desktop rendering and emulation are not physical-device evidence.

## Shipping approach
Ship bounded, tested batches to main under the user's standing authorization. Include behavior tests for substantive changes and visual checks for art/layout. Follow failures and concrete playtest findings before expanding scope. Keep this plan current as priorities change.
