# Aurelia’s chosen landing

October 13, 2026. [Issue #51](https://github.com/haydenwatkins/Nobodys-Quest/issues/51). Implementation and verification in an isolated checkout while Briar’s release code is frozen; publishing follows Briar. No release claim yet.

The player chooses where the boss arrives, escapes a committed flight, then uses the wind to approach. A real free attack chooses between feather landings. Actual reachable walking paths determine the choice window; Guardian help lengthens that window before the final escape warning. The landing and path stay fixed when committed. A painted capsule shows the complete 18-pixel swept contact radius. Native ground collision moves Aurelia along the marked flight; swept contact hurts only the actual crossing. After arrival the same corridor offers a short tailwind toward her. Normal walking remains stronger than the wind and can oppose it. No teleport, ward bypass, global counter stun/cancel or permanent walking tax.

The first phase teaches two approachable landings. Phase two puts the wind approach across the flight, with its own reachable start; the player leaves the dangerous flight and joins that crosswind. The final phase offers a close landing or a longer flight with a different escape/approach. Native terrain and the player’s reachable routes choose truthful alternatives; cramped ground can yield a short single landing rather than inventing an unreachable point. Removing unrelated stars/crescents keeps the flight decision central. The original 88 health, seven-point Sharp ward and native recoveries/ownership remain; safe completion and ordinary attack/ward interrupts retire transient choices honestly. Stun or a real body shove interrupts an unreleased/body flight, without cancelling pressure from another actor; already-released approach wind remains independent of body incapacitation.

The native Sky Mark gift, once-only star, restored wind lifts, courier discovery/outing and chosen return retain their existing consumers. Original restored-region behavior suppresses automatic boss respawn. Later bodies can revisit through the existing earned Manyfold pool. A warm repeat response remembers the landing or footwork and points home to the cliffpost; no new mode, menu control, currency or compulsory quest.

Verification observes real free attacks and ward separation, all phase/help decisions, arrival/ground collision, a real wind approach opposed by ordinary walking and phase/ward/travel/knockout cleanup. Layout review supplies earned-body/phase/initial-position fixtures and isolates Aurelia from ordinary regional foes to inspect each signature clearly. These captures establish input/presentation; complete native regional and Manyfold fights, appropriate regression, independent review and exact Pages/public checks are separate gates. Physical-device and continuous family playtesting remain unmeasured.
