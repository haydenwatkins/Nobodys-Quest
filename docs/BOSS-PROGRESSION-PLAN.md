# Boss progression delivery

October 13, 2026. This is the expanded implementation plan requested by the family, not a claim that the entire roster has shipped. `BOSS-IDENTITY.md` records current behavior and `CLOUD-ROADMAP.md` orders releases. Matt's installed implementation/TDD and independent review workflow applies to each bounded boss ticket. Existing attacks, walking, save/pickup and supported-input boundaries are the established verification seams.

A memorable encounter should leave the player able to describe an action and its consequence without the boss's name. Shared readable warnings, help, worn-form mastery and equal once-only gifts are retained. Every release must teach its decision in standard play, develop it through actual choices, and connect its gift to the next useful journey/return. Changes below are designs until their release evidence is recorded.

The designs may change when native geography or verification reveals a better decision. Record the reason and compare neighboring optimal play before replacing one. Keep every boss's identity independent of a mandatory prop, one selected form, a new menu, a new currency, count inflation or an unavoidable movement penalty.

Final acceptance includes an anonymous loop comparison for all nineteen definitions, meaningful phase development, native favorite/slow/free and walking answers, help combinations, reward collection/save/revisit and protected next use/return. Verify the entire progression through an earned campaign checkpoint sequence and review mixed builds. Automated completion does not establish physical-device readability or continuous family enjoyment; document those limits without making family playtesting a routine publishing gate.
