# Original cast and world direction

September 30, 2026: the user may publish this game and wants the systems inspired by form switching/mastery/mixing, while the characters must become an original cast. This applies to the protagonist, forms, bosses, NPCs, environments, lore and eventual branding. Cute, fairly detailed pixel art remains the visual direction.

Use the game's own strongest material: restored roads, living regional guardians, World Marks, keepsakes, homecoming, and small acts that wake the landscape. Build recognizable characters through a distinct silhouette, material, equipment, expression and role in that world. Common animal/class archetypes can be starting points, but a copied recognizable character with changed colors or name does not satisfy this brief. Do not reproduce reference sprites, portraits, costume designs or dialogue.
