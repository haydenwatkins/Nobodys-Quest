# Patchling's Quest

The family's 2D adventure: useful shapes, purposeful promises and playful guardian encounters.

## Language

**Form**: A body Patchling can wear, with its own useful arts and earned mastery.
_Avoid_: Class, skin

**Form outing**: A protected stretch of useful play with a newly discovered form before the next discovery competes for attention.

**Promise**: An optional request the player deliberately accepts from a neighbor, with a meaningful action and a good-news return.
_Avoid_: Automatically assigned chore

**Guardian help**: The independently chosen lantern that gives more time to recognize guardian dangers and supports retries.

**Heart recovery**: The independently chosen lantern that restores hearts after a breather.

**Guardian gift**: A lasting reward collected from the world after victory; repeat practice does not pay it again.

**Boss identity**: The distinctive decisions and consequences a fight offers, beyond its name, colors or props.

**Card passage**: Tansy's moving card fan, with an approach opened by splashing a landed pie while the rest of her act continues.

**Card Encore**: Tansy's deliberately chosen local practice variation with a following card passage; help and rewards remain independent.

**Mire channel**: Queen’s fixed lily-lined ground, made by breaking nearby landed crust. It shelters feet from her bubbles and reeds while her court continues fighting; other enemies keep their danger.

**Rebuilt ground**: Atlas’s small lasting stone crossings. They restore actual feet through later cracks; falling stones and ordinary projectiles remain dangerous.
